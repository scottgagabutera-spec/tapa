# Tapa — Brand Identity

> "Trust in motion. A stranger becomes your carrier. Distance becomes irrelevant."

## Brand Statement

Tapa is a premium, mobile-first peer-to-peer traveler delivery platform.
Every design decision must pass this test:

**Would engineers and designers at Airbnb, Uber, Grab, DHL, and Google Flights approve this?**

This is the GIANTS WAY standard.

## Brand Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Mobile first | Every screen is designed for mobile first, then expanded to web |
| User friendly | Nothing confusing. One action per screen. Obvious at a glance |
| Modern | Current design patterns. No outdated UI. Feels like 2026 and beyond |
| Premium | Feels expensive even when free. Quality in every pixel |
| Giants Way | Airbnb, Uber, Grab, DHL, Google Flights level of execution |
| Long term | Built to scale. Architecture and design that lasts years |
| Consistency | Same design language on every screen, every state, every platform |
| Unique | Tapa has its own identity. Not a clone of anything |

## Logo

Letter T formed by a travel route line ending in a destination pin.
Simple. Scalable. Works as app icon at 16px and billboard at 10 feet.

## Buttons
- Primary: Coral background, white text, 12px radius, 48px height on mobile
- Secondary: Transparent, coral border, coral text
- Ghost: No border, muted text
- Always full width on mobile

## Cards
- Background: #1A2F45
- Border: 1px #243B55
- Radius: 16px
- Shadow: 0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)

## Motion
- Fast: 150ms — micro interactions
- Base: 200ms — hover states
- Slow: 300ms — transitions
- Easing: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
